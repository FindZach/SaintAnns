About Our Lady of the Valley

Two churches, one parish family

Our Lady of the Valley Parish is a Catholic community in the Diocese of Rochester, formed from the historic parishes of St. Ann in Hornell and St. Mary in Rexville, New York.

We celebrate the sacraments, form disciples through faith formation for all ages, and serve our neighbors through more than a dozen active ministries.

Through Baptism, Reconciliation, Eucharist, Confirmation, Anointing, Matrimony and Holy Orders, we encounter Christ in the sacramental life of the Church.

Whether you are returning to the faith, searching, or lifelong Catholic — you are welcome at any of our Masses.
